Our family story

Leicester roots. Industry connections. Family values.

PK Sewing Machines was started in 1982 by two brothers and remains maintained by the family today. Over more than four decades, the business has built a respected reputation across the UK and long-standing contacts throughout the sewing-machine sector.

We work with domestic customers, tailors, alteration businesses, schools, workrooms and commercial manufacturers — combining traditional customer service with deep practical knowledge of modern and older machinery.

Family-run. Friendly. Fairly priced. And still testing machines on our own bench before they leave the shop.
